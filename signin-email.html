Sign in with email

The identity here is an email address, and any well-formed address is accepted as long as the password is right — so a run's own permanent address signs in without this page ever naming it. As on login.html, only a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 digest (150 000 iterations) ships in the source. Accounts created on signup.html are also accepted with their own password.
