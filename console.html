Billing internals

Every value on this page is deliberately out of reach of a normal step — painted into a canvas, hidden in ::before, sealed in a shadow root, or held only as a JS global. Each card carries the exact snippet to paste into a Custom-JS step, and a readout you can assert on afterwards. This is the one page in the playground that breaks the “live, self-describing DOM text” rule on purpose. In the product these are Custom actions (JavaScript) — types Custom action, Create variable, Custom validation — and imported values are read inside the snippet as variables.test['name'] / variables.data['name'] / variables.global['name'].
